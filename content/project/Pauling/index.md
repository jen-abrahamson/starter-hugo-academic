**PNNL Pauling Fellowship - Integrating Satellites, Physical Models, & Flux Towers**
&nbsp;

This project is funded as part of my Linus Pauling Fellowship at PNNL which seeks to figure out how to accurately map high-resolution, short term inundation dynamics and then investigate how that impacts our understanding of methane emissions from five different wetland ecosystems. This research is focused on mapping hydrologic dynamics at flux tower locations using GeoAI methods with satellite imagery and hydrologic modeling with the Advanced Terrestrial Simulator (ATS). This project is currently in progress, check back for updates!
